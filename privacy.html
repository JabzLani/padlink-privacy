PadLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

PadLink turns your phone into a remote control, keyboard and gamepad for your TV or computer. It has no accounts, no adverts, no in-app purchases and no analytics. This policy explains the small amount of information the app handles.

Who is responsible

PadLink is published by its independent developer. Privacy contact: jabulanimlaudzi@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

PadLink saves the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage. None of it is sent to us or to any server we run.

InformationWhy it is saved
The name and network address of TVs and devices you connect toSo you can reconnect with one tap
Pairing keys and permission tokens issued by your TVSo you do not have to approve the connection every time
Your settings: button mapping, quick-launch apps, themes, layout choicesTo remember your choices

Deleting the app or clearing its data removes all of this. You can also remove a single saved device from Settings.

Information sent to your TV or computer

When you press a button, type, move the trackpad or use the gamepad, that input is sent directly from your phone to the device you chose, over your own Wi-Fi network or over Bluetooth. It does not pass through any server of ours. PadLink looks for Android TVs on your Wi-Fi network (a local network search) so you can pick one from a list.

Permissions

PermissionWhy PadLink needs it
Internet and network stateTo reach TVs and devices on your local Wi-Fi network
Wi-Fi multicastTo find Android TVs on your network
Bluetooth (nearby devices)So your phone can appear as a Bluetooth keyboard, mouse or gamepad to a TV or PC you pair it with

PadLink does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Keeping the screen on

While PadLink is open it keeps the screen on so the controller does not lock in the middle of use. This does not collect any information.

Information Google Play provides

If you installed PadLink from Google Play, Google may give the developer anonymous technical reports, such as crash and performance statistics, from users who have chosen to share them. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. PadLink itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics software.

Sharing

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising, because we do not collect it. PadLink contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Security

Pairing keys are stored in the app's private storage and are not included in device backups. Control traffic goes only to the device you select on your own network. Some TV brands, such as Roku, accept unencrypted commands on the local network, so use PadLink on a network you trust.

Trademarks

PadLink is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google, Samsung, LG, Roku, Netflix, YouTube, Disney, Amazon, Apple, Spotify or any other brand named in the app. All names and trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to describe what PadLink works with.

Deleting your information

There are no accounts to delete and nothing is held on our servers. To remove what PadLink saved on your phone, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Children

PadLink is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
